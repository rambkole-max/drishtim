Four ways to draw sight

All built on drishti — vision. Site palette: vermilion #FF4D1C, acid #D8FF3E, ink #0A0A09, paper #EDEBE5. Each shown large, as a lockup, at 40px, and at 22px favicon size, on both grounds.

A

Aperture

Six iris blades. Literally the organ of sight, already the device on the site, and the hexagonal opening stays readable when tiny.

Serif wordmark option Drishtim

Strongest at small sizes. The solid hexagon survives a 16px favicon where linework wouldn't. Risk: aperture marks are common in photography branding — the six-blade geometry has to be precise to not read as generic.

B

Crop mark

Four corner brackets and nothing else. The square exists only in your head — the negative space is the logo.

The most ownable of the four. It's already the site's cursor and hero frame, so logo and interface become one idea. It also animates naturally — corners snap inward on load. Risk: without the dot it reads as a UI element rather than a logo.
