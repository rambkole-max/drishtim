Questions (6)

Everything worth knowing before the first call.

Do you work outside Latur?
Yes. We're based in Latur and work with brands across India. Shoots are planned at your location; everything else runs remotely.
Which platforms do you post to?
Instagram and Facebook — the two channels that actually sell in tier-2 Maharashtra. We'd rather run two properly than five thinly. Paid ads run across Meta, and Google Search where the category justifies it.
Is there a minimum term?
Monthly retainers run on a 3-month minimum so the content has time to prove itself. One-time packages have fixed scopes and no ongoing commitment. Most engagements start with a 3-month pilot.
Is ad spend included?
No, and that's deliberate. Your ad account stays in your name and you pay Meta or Google directly, so every rupee is visible to you daily. We manage strategy and creative; you keep control of the money.
What results can I expect?
Too early for us to claim an average — we're one client in. What we promise is visible weekly progress in a doc you own, and an honest conversation the moment something isn't working. Real numbers will live in the work section, not in a marketing claim.
How quickly can we start?
Typically within three to five working days of the first call, beginning with a discovery session on your brand, goals and calendar.
